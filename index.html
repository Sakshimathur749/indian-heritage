ABOUT THE INDIAN HERITAGE SCHOOL

A Place to Learn, Grow and Discover

Located in the peaceful and green surroundings of Bungal, Pathankot, at the foothills of Dalhousie, The Indian Heritage School provides a learning environment designed to support students beyond academics.

The school is a co-educational institution offering education from UKG to Class 12, with a focus on high academic standards, self-discipline, self-reliance, and holistic development.

The Classroom:

LEARNING BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

The Indian Heritage School is a place where achievements are celebrated, curiosity is explored, and dreams are inspired. Through a balanced blend of academics, creativity, and values, students are empowered to learn, grow, and lead with confidence and compassion.

Education That Goes Beyond Books

Students get opportunities to learn through modern classrooms, practical activities, sports, and co-curricular programmes.

Smart Learning

Digital smart classes, modern laboratories, a library with 2,000+ volumes, and facilities for art, craft, dance, and music, home science.

Academics

English-medium education from UKG to Class 12, supported by trained and qualified teachers, with Robotics and AI included in learning.

Sports & Activities

Facilities include a 400-metre track, hockey and football field, gym, yoga, basketball courts, volleyball courts, cricket, skating rink and a shooting range.

School Clubs

Students can explore their interests through 11 school clubs, including Arts, Computer, Science, Music, Photography, and Sports.
